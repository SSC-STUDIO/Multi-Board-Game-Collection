## About This Game

**Multi-Board-Game-Collection** focuses on two deep strategy classics — Gomoku and Go — with shared 3D tabletop rendering and a unified launcher.

### Why You'll Love It

- **Two Games, One Launcher** — Gomoku and Go with instant switching and consistent UI.
- **Immersive 3D** — Wood-grain boards, stone materials, soft shadows, drop animations, and audio SFX. One shared tabletop with three moods: Home, Park, and Competition.
- **AI That Teaches** — Three difficulty levels. Hard uses deeper search with opening books and heuristics. Optional QI Coach gives natural-language advice (Gomoku).
- **Zero Friction** — No ads, no tracking, no background services. Play on Web, Android, or Desktop.
- **Full Bilingual** — English and Simplified Chinese UI with keyboard accessibility and responsive layout.

### The Games

**Gomoku (Five in a Row)**
Classic 15×15 or 19×19 board. Standard rules or Renju forbidden-move enforcement. Opening theory, threat detection, and phase-aware AI search. Optional LLM QI Coach for move suggestions and post-game review.

**Go (Weiqi / Baduk)**
Territory play on 9×9, 13×13, or 19×19. Chinese and Japanese scoring with life-and-death evaluation. AI uses minimax with Monte Carlo territory evaluation. Post-game territory visualization.
